I am an Associate Professor with a PhD in Applied Mathematics, specializing in Control and Optimization. My research focuses on developing and applying mathematical and computational methods, artificial intelligence, and machine learning to address complex scientific and real-world problems. My research interests include mathematical modeling, optimization, intelligent systems, climate science, and data-driven decision-making, with an emphasis on interdisciplinary research and practical applications.
